Tikka Masala

Medium heat · Smoky, creamy, a little sweet

Ingredients & allergens

Tomatoes (40%), onion, cream (milk), butter (milk), ginger, garlic, rapeseed oil, kasuri methi, garam masala, kashmiri chilli, cumin, coriander, turmeric, sea salt, cane sugar. Contains milk. Made in a kitchen that also handles nuts.

How to cook with it

Brown 500 g chicken, paneer or chickpeas in a pan. Pour in the whole jar, simmer 10 minutes, finish with a spoon of butter or cream. Serve with rice or naan.

Why glass

Why the jar
mattersZeroPlasticglass only

The jar is the last step of the recipe. Sealing the sauce hot in glass keeps the oils, the aroma and the colour exactly where we left them.

Freshness

Vacuum-sealed at 85°C straight from the kettle, so every jar opens with the same bright tomato snap as the first ladle.

Aroma & flavor

Glass doesn't absorb smells or give them back. Kasuri methi and smoked chilli stay loud until the lid comes off.

Authentic legacy

The recipe our grandmother cooked in a brass handi, once measured by eye and now to the gram.

Kesar vs. the usual jar

Most supermarket sauces are cooked fast and thickened with starch. Ours takes three hours and doesn't need help.

How spicy is it?

Medium. Kashmiri chilli gives colour and warmth more than burn. Most kids are fine with it, and you can stir in extra cream to soften it further.

How long does an opened jar last?

Five days in the fridge with the lid on. Unopened jars keep for 12 months in the cupboard because they're sealed hot, with no preservatives added.

Is it vegetarian? Gluten-free?

It's vegetarian and contains no gluten ingredients. It does contain milk (cream and butter), so it isn't vegan.
